Morocco Through My Lens
March 15, 2024  ·  8 min read  ·  Marrakech · Fes

The medina doesn't make sense until it does. For the first two hours, every alley looks the same — terracotta walls, sudden turns, the smell of cumin mixed with exhaust. Then something shifts.

I found the spice souk by accident, following a man carrying saffron on his shoulders. Stall after stall of turmeric mountains and rose-petal pyramids, each vendor with a different theory about what makes Moroccan cuisine what it is.
